## Exact checklist excerpts independently paraphrased from book
Oxford IB Prepared AA §1.3 states the reader should know power laws, the definition and laws of logarithms, and be able to manipulate exponents, change log base and solve log equations. This is closely aligned to the **23 Nov Pre-IB exam**. It does NOT add complex numbers, trigonometry or calculus to the school SAS scope.

Biology Oxford IB Prepared (for first assessment 2025) gives command terms such as state/outline/explain/discuss and differentiates depth of response. Use the framework to practise explaining *why*, while marking according to the school's 5 essay questions rather than substituting IB Diploma grading.
