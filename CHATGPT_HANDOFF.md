## 23 Nov correction — learner's preferred quadratic factoring method
The learner corrected a tutoring explanation calling monic division 'awkward'. They were remembering **slide-and-divide / AC factorisation**: $ax^2+bx+c\to x^2+bx+ac=(x+m)(x+n)$, with $m+n=b$, $mn=ac$, then restore $a(x+m/a)(x+n/a)$ (or $(ax+m)(ax+n)/a$) and simplify. Demonstrated $6x^2+11x+3\to(x+9)(x+2)\to 6(x+9/6)(x+2/6)=(2x+3)(3x+1)$. Important caveat intermediate quadratic is not the original. **Keep this technique in future teaching; do not assume the learner means simply divide all polynomial coefficients by $a$.** The learner still wants to be taught before being tested.
