## Priority algorithm
1. **Exam tomorrow:** high-priority retrieval, worked written answers, short timed mock.
2. **Exam in 2 days:** main conceptual coverage, examples and transfer tasks.
3. **Language memory-heavy subject (Arabic):** spaced practice from day 1; target daily recall rather than a single cram.
4. **Exam in 3+ days:** incremental low-load foundation and retrieval.
5. If student struggles on prerequisites, briefly return to the regular curriculum baseline, then test transfer back to Pre-IB.

## Daily stopping criteria (per core subject)
- State the learning objectives from memory.
- Solve at least one straightforward and one transfer problem without hints.
- Explain errors and perform a fresh correction.
- For writing, produce an original response and revise it against a rubric.
