## Measurable Pre-IB objectives

### Mathematics
M1. Prove and apply index rules with stated domain conditions; transform exponential equations with substitutions and validate candidates.
M2. Link tables/graphs, base and transformation parameters; interpret domain/range/asymptotes/intercepts using Desmos as a CHECK.
M3. Use logarithm identities, solve equations with argument restrictions and check for extraneous solutions.
M4. Factor or solve quadratics; explain discriminants, Vieta sums/products, reconstruct polynomials after transforming roots; sketch the vertex, intercepts and axis.
M5. Formulate constraints, find a feasible region, evaluate vertices and explain optimal solution and its context. Know how SPLTV differs from two-variable inequality optimization.
M6. Present **essay solutions**: define, substitute, show algebra, state restrictions, conclude and verify.

### Bahasa Indonesia
I1. Identify orientasi, peristiwa/masalah and reorientasi in biography, and **explain their effect** on portrayal of a figure.
I2. Analyze adjectives, temporal connectives, reported/direct speech and figurative language with evidence.
I3. Judge whether an LHO objectively reports observations, and separate evidence from unsupported recommendations.
I4. Identify ethos (credibility), pathos (emotion), logos (reason), but **evaluate effectiveness using quotations**.
I5. Write a coherent **TBEM** paragraph: Topik (claim), Bukti (precise quoted evidence), Elaborasi (mechanism/interpretation), Makna (implication).

### Biology
B1. Compare genetic, species and ecosystem biodiversity; apply to Indonesia and conservation choices.
B2. Apply hierarchical classification and dichotomous keys to distinguish organisms; explain why taxonomic ideas change with evidence.
B3. Compare prokaryotic/eukaryotic cells, explain bacteria/archaea similarities and differences, asexual reproduction and gene transfer without conflating them.
B4. Label/explain viral structures and predict outcomes of lytic vs lysogenic infection; relate roles to specific evidence.
B5. Write a scientific essay with correct terms, causal reasoning, clear comparisons, labeled diagrams and uncertainty where appropriate.

### English
E1. Read and infer from descriptive, procedure, narrative texts using textual evidence.
E2. Select grammar based on timeline, aspect, voice, agreement and meaning rather than memorizing trigger words.
E3. Draft an audience-appropriate 200–250-word blog/magazine article with title, lead, coherent paragraphs, accurate grammar and a purposeful ending.
E4. Revise for clarity, register, transitions and errors under exam conditions.
