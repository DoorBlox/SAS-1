# SAS Fikih X 2026/2027 — KOMPAS Teaching Companion

**Status:** Directly inspected the uploaded HTML KOMPAS Rangkuman Materi SAS Fikih Kelas X Ganjil 2026/2027: official-looking school Stellaris/Divisi Akademik dan Literasi KOMPAS original, six complete chapters, a 6-row content blueprint and 20 MCQ sample questions with answer discussions. **School scope: 50 multiple choice questions.** This is original explanatory paraphrase, not a republication of the source.

**Important source boundary:** The content is broadly framed in Shafi'i jurisprudence (mazhab Syafi'i) and may differ across madhhabs. In exam preparation use the school's KOMPAS distinctions while separately noting current law if official legal details changed; do not treat every technical rule as an absolute all-madhhab rule. The learner's standing preference is **no tests until opt-in**.

## Bab 1 — Thaharah (najis, hadas, water, istinja, wudhu, ghusl, tayammum)
- **Najis** is a substance whose presence on body/clothing/place can affect prayer validity; **hadas** is a ritual state that requires purification. Cleaning a stained shirt removes najis but does not itself lift hadas; ablution removes hadas kecil but may not clean najis on clothing.
- **Air mutlak** pure and purifying; musyammas in traditional school presentation; musta'mal and mutaghayyar are classed as pure but not purifying; mutanajjis is impure. Threshold **dua qullah** has juristic variations; KOMPAS discusses examples 216–270 litres.
- **Najis mukhaffafah**: exclusively breastfed boy under 2, urine, sprinkle water; **mutawassithah**: urine/blood/feces etc., wash until the traces are removed; **mughallazhah**: dog/pig, wash seven times including once with clean earth under Shafi'i approach.
- **Najis ainiyah** substance remains perceptible; **hukmiyah** its substance and properties no longer visible but legal washing required.
- **Hadas kecil**: normally wudhu; examples bodily discharges, loss of consciousness, Shafi'i rules on unrelated adult opposite-sex skin touch and touching private parts. **Hadas besar**: ghusl; janabah, menstruation/childbirth-related causes.
- **Six essential wudhu steps in KOMPAS**: intention; wash face; wash arms through elbows; wipe part of head; wash feet through ankles; sequential order.
- **Ghusl essentials**: intention + water over the whole body (including hair/skin).
- **Tayammum** when water unavailable/harmful: clean earth/dust, intention, face, hands to elbows, order per textbook. Common school rule 1 tayammum per obligatory prayer in Shafi'i approach; return to water when available before praying if excuse was absence.
- **Exam reasoning:** distinguish *what is invalid* vs *what is merely makruh*, *najasah* vs *hadath* and *fardhu* vs *sunnah*.

## Bab 2 — Salat and rukhsah
- **Syarat wajib** = conditions for being obligated; **syarat sah** = prerequisites of valid execution; **rukun** = necessary parts of prayer; **sunnah ab'ad** if omitted traditionally merits sujud sahwi, unlike **sunnah hai'at**.
- **13 rukuns listed KOMPAS:** niat, stand if able, takbiratul ihram, Fatihah, ruku with calmness, itidal with calmness, 2 prostrations with calmness, sit between prostrations calmly, final sitting, final tashahhud, salawat after final tashahhud, first salam, order (some texts count tuma'ninah separately).
- **Sujud sahwi** two prostrations for certain mistakes/forgetfulness, e.g. omitted early tashahhud; an omitted **rukun** must be restored, not replaced with sujud sahwi alone.
- **Jamak** combine only Dhuhr+Asr or Maghrib+Isha. **Taqdim** first prayer's time, **ta'khir** second prayer's time. Subuh not paired.
- **Qashar** 4->2 rakats for Dhuhr, Asr, Isha if eligible travel, never Maghrib or Subuh. School version two marhalah approximately 81–89km and other travel conditions. **Jamak and qashar are different concessions:** jamak affects scheduling, qashar affects rakats.
- **Jamak-qashar Maghrib+Isha** = 3+2=5, not 2+2. Dhuhr+Asr =2+2=4.
- **Sick-person prayer**: standing if able -> sitting -> lying on side -> lying on back -> sign as able; worship obligation remains with awareness subject to juristic caveats.
- **What to notice in case questions:** travel legality, distance, whether still traveling, time of prayer, which prayers, imam status, able to stand or sit.

## Bab 3 — Zakat: fitrah, mal, asnaf, law
- **Fitrah** = one sha' of staple food, KOMPAS educational approximation **2.5 kg rice per person**. Calculate dependents and time (prefer before Eid prayer).
- **Mal**: nisab minimum threshold; haul ownership period when applicable; rate depends on asset.
- **Gold** >=85g for one lunar year: 2.5% on taxable quantity. **Silver** >=595g: 2.5%. Trade inventory+cash+current receivables less due obligations, compare with nisab, then 2.5% in KOMPAS worked model.
- **Agriculture** >= approximate 5 wasaq per KOMPAS: 10% natural/rain-fed, 5% paid irrigation, due at harvest.
- **Rikaz** 20% in school's presentation. Some other types follow differing conditions.
- **2026 income zakat threshold**, VERIFIED official BAZNAS press 24 Feb 2026: Rp91,681,728/year or Rp7,640,144/month, rate 2.5%. Source https://baznas.go.id/news-show/BAZNAS_RI_Tetapkan_Nisab_Zakat_Penghasilan_dan_Jasa_2026_Rp7.640.144_per_bulan/3777.
- **Eight asnaf**: fakir, miskin, amil, muallaf, riqab, gharim, fi sabilillah, ibnu sabil. Case: somebody unable to repay a permissible medical bill -> gharim. Stranded traveller -> ibnu sabil.
- **UU 23/2011**: BAZNAS national body; LAZ community-founded permitted agency; UPZ collection units organised by BAZNAS. The law governs accountable management, whereas fiqh determines the religious duty, amounts and eligible recipients.
- **Source sample calculations:** 120g eligible gold -> 3g zakat; irrigated 4000kg -> 200kg; 5 people -> 12.5kg staple food.

## Bab 4 — Jenazah
- **Fardhu kifayah:** washing, shrouding, funeral prayer and burial; obligation discharged if enough people undertake it.
- **Memandikan:** discreet, same-sex or valid exceptions; wash away impurities and whole body; special martyrs and severely damaged bodies have separate rules.
- **Mengafani:** minimum whole body; KOMPAS teaches sunnah 3 layers for men, 5 for women.
- **Funeral prayer** no rukuk/sujud; 4 takbirs: 1 Al-Fatihah; 2 salawat; 3 prayer for deceased; 4 further supplication; then salam. Imam stands by head for deceased man and midbody for deceased woman in Shafi'i account.
- **Burial** right side facing qibla. Takziah is comfort/support for bereaved; distinguishes from inappropriate mourning practices.

## Bab 5 — Halal slaughter, certification, aqiqah, qurban
- **Standard halal slaughter in KOMPAS:** slaughterer/animal/instrument/method; animal lawful and alive; sharp blade not teeth/nails; sever **hulqum** (airway) and **mari'** (food passage), with **wadajain** vessels cut for fuller procedure. Humane handling and correct religious intent matter.
- **Stunning** can be conditional on reversibility/no prior death per MUI teaching material; case questions ask whether animal is alive at actual halal cut.
- **Roles:** BPJPH issues halal certificate; LPH inspects; fatwa-authorised body establishes halal determination; Juru Sembelih Halal implements competence. Do not confuse BPJPH with BAZNAS.
- **LEGALITY CORRECTION compared with KOMPAS HTML:** KOMPAS still names **PP No.39/2021**. Official JDIH BPK establishes PP39/2021 **REVOKED** by **PP No.42/2024**, and JDIH BPJPH confirms PP42/2024 later **AMENDED** by **PP No.40/2026**. Do not present PP39/2021 as current. Primary links: https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/304896/pp-no-42-tahun-2024 ; https://jdih.halal.go.id/jdih/dokumen/ccbd84f4-14f5-47a6-8b91-7d6ae1333c75.
- **Aqiqah** for birth: commonly day 7, 2 sheep/goats for boy, 1 for girl, meat preferably cooked.
- **Qurban** 10–13 Dhu al-Hijjah, certain livestock of proper age and condition; a cow or camel may be shared by up to 7 people, a goat/sheep by 1; qurban distribution usually raw. Nazar changes its status.
- **Case focus:** distinguish aqiqah vs qurban time, intent, number, and distribution; understand when slaughter is legally halal, not merely certificate paperwork.

## Bab 6 — Haji, umrah and current situations
- **Haji** obligatory once for those who meet ability criteria (*istitha'ah*), during specific hajj period; **umrah** available year-round, obligatory once according to Shafi'i view, other madhhabs differ.
- **Hajj rukun per KOMPAS:** ihram (niat), wukuf at Arafah, tawaf ifadah, sa'i, tahallul, tertib. Omitting a rukun leaves hajj incomplete/invalid until rectified where possible — dam does not simply replace it.
- **Wajib hajj:** ihram from miqat, mabit at Muzdalifah/Mina, jamarat, tawaf wada, avoiding ihram prohibitions. Omission without valid excuse requires dam while hajj is otherwise valid.
- **Umrah** no wukuf/Mina/jamarat; rukun includes ihram, tawaf, sa'i, tahallul, tertib.
- **Ifrad:** hajj then umrah separately; typically no tamattu/qiran dam. **Tamattu:** umrah then exit ihram, later hajj, dam. **Qiran:** one combined ihram for hajj+umrah, dam.
- **Tawaf** 7 circles, Kaaba on left. **Sa'i** 7 crossings Safa->Marwah ending at Marwah. **Wukuf** 9 Dhu al-Hijjah essential.
- **Special cases:** woman menstruating may not perform tawaf during menstruation; tawaf ifadah cannot be omitted, tawaf wada is excused. Long queue, unlicensed visas, health and ambulatory safari wukuf, disabled pilgrims, murur and tanazul, badal hajj (eligible substitute already completed own hajj).
- **Current hajj law** UU No.14/2025 (third amendment to UU 8/2019), official https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/331538/uu-no-14-tahun-2025. General KOMPAS description has been verified.

## Cases from uploaded KOMPAS MCQ examples — teaches the reasoning, not a test
- Breastfed baby boy under 2 urine -> mukhaffafah, sprinkle.
- Unexpected clean water before prayer after tayammum for lack of water -> use wudhu.
- Missing early tashahhud vs missing a rukun -> sujud sahwi vs restore rukun.
- Travel 150km Maghrib and Isha combined in Isha time -> jamak ta'khir, 3+2=5 if qashar conditions.
- Sick person unable to stand/sit -> side lying if able.
- Farmer 4000kg on paid irrigation -> 5%=200kg.
- Jenazah 4 takbirs -> after third, pray for deceased.
- Halal certificate -> BPJPH, zakat institution -> BAZNAS.
- Miss Muzdalifah without excuse -> hajj valid, dam, because it is **wajib**, not rukun.
- Menstruating pilgrim after wukuf yet to do tawaf ifadah -> must await purity, not replace tawaf with dam.
- **Do not assume all 20 examples represent the exact 50-item SAS**, only types in a source-aligned revision corpus.

## Memorisation approach
Classify questions by *status and consequence*, not isolated facts:
- Najis vs hadas; small vs major; wajib vs sunnah.
- Syarat sah vs rukun vs sunnah; what invalidates vs what requires sujud sahwi.
- Jamak (time) vs qashar (rakats).
- Nisab (threshold) vs haul (time) vs kadar (percentage); muzakki vs mustahik.
- Four funeral obligations and exact four-takbir sequence.
- Aqiqah (birth) vs qurban (Idul Adha).
- Hajj **rukun** missing -> cannot replace with dam; hajj **wajib** missing -> dam may apply.
- Semantics of legal current sources vs textbook print (PP39/2021 no longer current).

**No surprise quizzes:** teach all six chapters before optional practice.
