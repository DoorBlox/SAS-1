# Pre-IB SAS Mathematics: authentic IB Alignment and Assessment Practice

**Reviewed 22 November 2026. Grade X-6 Pre-IB. Mathematics SAS: 23 Nov 2026.**

## What determines study priorities?
1. **School's Pre-IB 13-page SAS master kisi-kisi**: exponents, logarithms, quadratic equations/functions including transformed roots/Vieta, SPLTV and linear programming (systems of inequalities, graphing feasible regions, optimizing). Reported live exam: **15 essays**. School-specific objectives win over textbook breadth.
2. **Teacher practice and formative with markscheme**: calibrate multi-part explanations, graph interpretations and proof/justification; some tasks contain errors—flag rather than obey.
3. **Regular SAS materials, CCC practice**: the foundation, NOT the ceiling.
4. **Genuine IB DP Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches guide and Pearson/Oxford**: conceptual teaching, logical mathematical reasoning, inquiry, mathematical notation, command terms and multi-step mark allocation. School **Pre-IB is not an official IB Diploma examination**. Do not automatically import every DP HL topic.
5. **Original learner-specific notes**: intuitive explanations, derivations, worked examples, memory aids, and teacher-calibrated original practice. **TEACH before testing**.

## 2021 IB Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches Guide, November 2024 update
User uploaded the official 101-PDF-page guide. Printed pages and objectives verified:
- **Assessment objectives pp. 23–24:** knowledge/understanding, problem solving, communication/interpretation, technology, reasoning, inquiry. IB expects mathematical arguments, accurate diagrams and interpretation, not only numeric answers.
- **Prior learning pp. 26–27:** algebra/factorising; powers including rational exponents (HL prior-learning designation); surds and rationalising; quadratic equations; graphs, inequalities; linear systems.
- **SL1.5 pp. 30–31 and SL1.7 pp. 31–32:** exponent laws, rational exponents, logarithm meaning/laws/change of base, exponent equations.
- **SL2.1–2.4 pp. 37–38:** straight lines, functions/domain/range, labels and sketches, intercepts/asymptotes.
- **SL2.6–2.7 p. 39:** quadratic three forms, zeros, vertex/axis, discriminant, quadratic equations/inequalities.
- **SL2.9–2.11 pp. 40–41:** exponential and logarithmic graphs, inverses, equations and transformations.
- **AHL1.16 pp. 35–36:** solutions of at most three equations in three unknowns. This aligns with school SPLTV, *not* proof that all methods/linear programming are official DP AA topics.
- **AHL2.12 p. 42:** root-sum/product generalisations.
- **Glossary pp. 89–90:** distinguish command terms (*state*, *find*, *show that*, *hence*, *explain*, *justify*, *sketch*, *verify*).
Do not assume official IB exam calculator, formula-booklet or paper regulations apply to the school SAS; obtain school-specific confirmation if relevant.

## Oxford IB Prepared Mathematics AA (Paul Belcher, Ed Kemp, 2021) — BODY INSPECTED
First Drive book collection: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tiy72IH5Z3nBf0CG2tcerWGhwuq6gw2a/view
- **§1.3, pp. 13–16:** exponent rules and logs as powers; log laws, inverses, change of base and equation solving. Notable annotated student error: rejecting a *negative log output*—the log output may be negative; the real-log *argument* must be positive.
- **§2.3, pp. 49 onward:** quadratic properties/graphs, discriminant, transformed roots, equations and inequalities, modelling examples.
- **§2.4, pp. 60 onward:** vertical and horizontal shifts, reflections, stretches and order. Annotated incorrect horizontal-shift direction; verify with a known point.
- **Introduction, pp. iv–vii:** learning-objective checklists, explicit “must know / should be able to,” worked problems, exam tips, annotated wrong and corrected student answers, revision and appropriate command terms.
- **Practice exams p. 197 onward:** IB-style M1 Method, A1 Accuracy/Achievement, R1 Reasoning and AG Answer Given; grade according to the specific question, not a flat generic score.

## Source quality warning: teacher quadratic formative
Teacher's 15-question formative and associated **92-mark M/A/R markscheme** contain explicit teacher notes identifying errors:
- **Q1:** $x^2-2x+5=0$ has discriminant $-16$, hence **no real roots**; ordering roots $x_1\le x_2$ in the reals is not defined.
- **Q7:** fixed real-number difference $10$ yields a product with a finite **minimum** but **no maximum**; do not silently change "maximum" to "minimum" without telling student.
- **Q9:** diagram's $x/y$ axis labels are swapped; correctly identify horizontal vs vertical axes.
These are key real IB-style habits: **check mathematical validity, don't blindly trust a markscheme.**
