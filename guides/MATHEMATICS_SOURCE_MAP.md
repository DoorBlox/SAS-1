## Source QA warnings
- Any negative log output is allowed if its *argument* is positive; rejecting a logarithm because it is negative is wrong (Oxford annotated example).
- Uploaded quadratic formative Q1 asks for ordered real roots of $x^2-2x+5=0$, impossible because $\Delta=-16$; teacher markscheme confirms. Q7 asks for maximum product at fixed difference, impossible over all real numbers; teacher markscheme explains intended minimum. Q9 mislabeled graph axes. Flag errors honestly before teaching.
- Extraction from some scanned/badly encoded PDFs drops minus signs and exponents; **check original visual page** for complex expressions.
- Do not put the PDFs, pages, markscheme or personal information in public GitHub. All teaching notes/practice are original.
