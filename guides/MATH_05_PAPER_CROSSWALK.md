# Mathematics SAS practice-paper crosswalk & marking guide

**Purpose:** a retrieval route from the 2026 Grade X-6 SAS topics to the *student's actual material*. This is an index and original practice design, not a copy or distribution of the uploaded works.

## Governing priorities
- **Actual Grade X-6 school Pre-IB blueprint:** 15 essay items, exponent/log, quadratic, SPLTV/linear programming; exact subtopics in SYLLABUS_MATRIX.md.
- **IB Prep teacher practice:** *Practice_Paper_SAS_Mathematics_IB_Prep_V1*, 20 multipart questions, **160 marks**, several explicit no-calculator questions. It is a PRACTICE PAPER and does not cover school linear programming.
- **Teacher quadratic formative:** *FORMATIVE_Quadratic_Equations* (15 questions), 92 total marks in supplied *MARKSCHEME_Quadratic_Equations.docx*. This is valuable evidence for method (M), accuracy (A), reasoning (R), follow-through (FT), answer given (AG).
- **Regular baseline:** CCC's *LATIHAN SOAL SAS EKSPONEN & LOGARITMA FINAL* (32 MCQs). These skills must be secure but are NOT the Pre-IB upper ceiling.
- **Additional new Maths folder** with exponent+log formative (90 min), long quadratic question bank, three extra practice banks, SPLTV and SPtLDV worksheets. See [full index](MATHEMATICS_SOURCE_MAP.md).

### Correction notes identified in the teacher-provided markscheme
- Quadratic formative **Q1**: $x^2-2x+5$ has discriminant $-16$; there is no ordering of real roots.
- Quadratic formative **Q7**: product of two arbitrary reals with difference 10 has no finite maximum (it has a minimum $-25$).
- Quadratic formative **Q9**: diagram axes are incorrectly labelled.
The markscheme is a useful source of rubric intent but not infallible. Learner should not be penalized for noticing mathematical flaws.

## How to practise an IB-style paper — only AFTER taught
1. Read the question's command term ("state" versus "show that" versus "hence"), variable domain and calculator rules.
2. Explain in one sentence why the selected method fits.
3. Show every meaningful transformation, not only calculator outputs.
4. Check domain, all roots, constraints, graph labels and physical context/units.
5. Use feedback analogous to M1/A1/R1/FT where the actual markscheme permits; if we create our own rubric, explicitly say it is NOT a school grade.
6. After a fully guided example, learner solves an unseen original question; record actual evidence in MASTERY_TRACKER.md.

**Never present questions before basic topic teaching.** For the full actual lessons see [Exponents](MATH_01_EXPONENTS_SURDS.md), [Logarithms](MATH_02_LOGARITHMS.md), [Quadratics](MATH_03_QUADRATICS.md), [Linear programming](MATH_04_LINEAR_PROGRAMMING.md).
