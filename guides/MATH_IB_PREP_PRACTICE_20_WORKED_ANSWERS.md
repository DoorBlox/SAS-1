## Q1 — Fractional indices
(a) $125^{2/3}=(5^3)^{2/3}=\boxed{25}$. (b) $(27/8)^{-2/3}=((3/2)^3)^{-2/3}=(2/3)^2=\boxed{4/9}$. Denominator of fractional exponent gives root; negative exponent gives reciprocal.

## Q2 — Common-base equations
(a) $3^m=9=3^2$, $3^n=27=3^3$, hence $\boxed{m=2,n=3}$.
(b) $9^{x+4}=27^{2x-1}\Rightarrow3^{2x+8}=3^{6x-3}\Rightarrow 2x+8=6x-3\Rightarrow\boxed{x=11/4}$.

## Q3 — Exact rectangle geometry
Dimensions $4+\sqrt3$ and $4-\sqrt3$ cm.
(a) Area $(4+\sqrt3)(4-\sqrt3)=16-3=\boxed{13\ {\rm cm}^2}$.
(b) Perimeter $2(8)=\boxed{16\ {\rm cm}}$.
(c) Diagonal $d^2=(4+\sqrt3)^2+(4-\sqrt3)^2=38$, so $\boxed{d=\sqrt{38}\ {\rm cm}}$.

## Q4 — Reciprocal-identity proof
(a) $(\sqrt x-1/\sqrt x)^2=x-2+1/x$ for $x>0$, by $(a-b)^2$.
(b) Given $\sqrt x-1/\sqrt x=2$, square to get $4=x-2+1/x$, hence $\boxed{x+1/x=6}$.
(c) $x^2+1/x^2=(x+1/x)^2-2=36-2=\boxed{34}$.

## Q5 — Similar envelopes and halves
C0 area $2^{1/4}$; initial aspect ratio longer/shorter $\sqrt2$.
(a) $\sqrt2\,w^2=2^{1/4}\Rightarrow w^2=2^{-1/4}\Rightarrow\boxed{w=2^{-1/8}\ {\rm m}}$.
(b) After halving the longer dimension, new sides are $w$ and $w/\sqrt2$, so ratio $\boxed{\sqrt2}$.
(c) C4 area $2^{1/4}/2^4=\boxed{2^{-15/4}\ {\rm m}^2}$.
(d) C0 longer side $L_0=\sqrt2\,2^{-1/8}=2^{3/8}$. Each shape-preserving halving reduces linear dimensions by $2^{-1/2}$. Four steps give $L_4=2^{3/8}(2^{-1/2})^4=\boxed{2^{-13/8}\ {\rm m}}$.
(e) C1→C4 has 3 halvings; $P_1/P_4=(\sqrt2)^3=\boxed{2\sqrt2}$, i.e. $2\sqrt2:1$.

## Q6 — Logarithms to solve exponentials
$25^{x+3}=15^{2x}\Rightarrow 5^{2x+6}=3^{2x}5^{2x}\Rightarrow5^6=3^{2x}$.
Take logs: $6\log5=2x\log3\Rightarrow\boxed{x=3\log5/\log3}$.

## Q7 — Telescoping logarithmic chain
$p=\log_{63}64\,\log_{62}63\cdots\log_2 3$.
Change base and cancel consecutive $\ln$ factors: $p=\ln64/\ln2=\log_2 64=\boxed6$.

## Q8 — Log equation
$\log_3x-\log_34=3+\log_35$. Domain $x>0$.
$\log_3(x/4)=\log_3(27\cdot5)=\log_3135\Rightarrow x/4=135\Rightarrow\boxed{x=540}$.

## Q9 — Vieta and transformed roots
Original roots $\alpha,\beta$ of $x^2-6x+3=0$.
(a) $\boxed{\alpha+\beta=6,\ \alpha\beta=3}$.
(b) $\alpha^2+\beta^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-2\alpha\beta=36-6=\boxed{30}$.
(c) Squared roots have sum 30, product 9; $\boxed{t^2-30t+9=0}$.
(d) $(\alpha-\beta)^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-4\alpha\beta=36-12=\boxed{24}$; $\boxed{|\alpha-\beta|=2\sqrt6}$.
(e) Since $\alpha\beta=3$, $1/\beta=\alpha/3$; the new roots are $4\alpha/3,4\beta/3$, sum 8, product 16/3. Equation $\boxed{3t^2-24t+16=0}$.

## Q10 — Parameter-discriminant condition
$f(x)=3px^2+(3p-4)x+p-2$ with $p\in\mathbb Q,\ p\ne0$.
(a) $\Delta=(3p-4)^2-4(3p)(p-2)=\boxed{16-3p^2}$.
(b) Distinct real quadratic roots require $\Delta>0$: $-4/\sqrt3<p<4/\sqrt3$, **and $p\ne0$, $p\in\mathbb Q$**. Thus $\boxed{p\in\mathbb Q\cap[(-4/\sqrt3,0)\cup(0,4/\sqrt3)]}$, where brackets only group the union (each endpoint is excluded).

## Q11 — Vertex and range
$3x^2+12x+5=3[(x+2)^2-4]+5=\boxed{3(x+2)^2-7}$.
(b) Vertex $\boxed{(-2,-7)}$. (c) Since $a=3>0$, range $\boxed{[-7,\infty)}$.

## Q12 — Read the ACTUAL graph (page 2)
The two x-intercepts shown are $(-3,0)$ and $(2,0)$; vertex $(-0.5,-12.5)$.
(a) Axis $\boxed{x=-1/2}$.
(b) $f(x)=a(x+3)(x-2)$; substitute $x=-1/2$: $-12.5=a(2.5)(-2.5)=-6.25a$, so $a=2$. In vertex form $f(x)=2(x+1/2)^2-25/2$, hence $\boxed{a=2,\ h=-1/2,\ k=-25/2}$.

## Q13 — Projectile
$h(t)=-5t^2+30t+35$, physical time $t\ge0$.
(a) Initial height $h(0)=\boxed{35\ {\rm m}}$.
(b) $\boxed{h(t)=-5(t-3)^2+80}$.
(c) Maximum $\boxed{80\ {\rm m}\text{ at }t=3\ {\rm s}}$.
(d) $h(t)=0\Rightarrow t^2-6t-7=(t-7)(t+1)=0$. Discard negative time. $\boxed{t=7\ {\rm s}}$.
(e) $h>60\Rightarrow t^2-6t+5<0\Rightarrow(t-1)(t-5)<0$, so $1<t<5$. Duration $\boxed{4\ {\rm s}}$.

## Q14 — Three-sided lifeguard fence, rope 80 m
(a) Rope sides $x,x$ and $80-2x$: $\boxed{A=x(80-2x)}$; $0<x<40$.
(b) $\boxed{A=-2(x-20)^2+800}$.
(c) $\boxed{A_{\max}=800\ {\rm m}^2\text{ at }x=20\ {\rm m}}$.
(d) $600=-2(x-20)^2+800\Rightarrow(x-20)^2=100$, giving $\boxed{x=10\text{ or }30\ {\rm m}}$.
(e) $A\ge600\iff(x-20)^2\le100$, giving $\boxed{10\le x\le30}$, within physical domain.

## Q15 — Plot the quadratic on the ACTUAL blank grid (page 3)
$f(x)=\tfrac14x^2+bx+c$, axis $x=2$, one root $(-2,0)$.
(a) Draw line $x=\boxed2$.
(b)(i) Second root is reflected through $x=2$: $B=\boxed{(6,0)}$.
(b)(ii) Factor $f(x)=\tfrac14(x+2)(x-6)=\tfrac14x^2-x-3$, hence $\boxed{b=-1,\ c=-3}$.
(c) Label A(-2,0) and B(6,0).
(d) Vertex $V=(2,f(2))=\boxed{(2,-4)}$.
(e) $C=(0,c)=\boxed{(0,-3)}$.
(f) Sketch upward-opening parabola through A, B, V, C and the reflected point (4,-3), symmetric about $x=2$.

## Q16 — Rationalise and simplify surds
(a) $\frac{12}{3-\sqrt5}\frac{3+\sqrt5}{3+\sqrt5}=\frac{12(3+\sqrt5)}4=\boxed{9+3\sqrt5}$.
(b) $\sqrt{48}+\sqrt{75}-\sqrt{27}=4\sqrt3+5\sqrt3-3\sqrt3=\boxed{6\sqrt3}$.

## Q17 — Logarithms in terms of given variables
Given $\log_a 2=x,\ \log_a 3=y$ for $a>0,\ a\ne1$.
(a) $\log_a12=\log_a(2^2\cdot3)=\boxed{2x+y}$.
(b) $\log_a(27/4)=\log_a(3^3/2^2)=\boxed{3y-2x}$.
(c) $\log_a\sqrt{18a}=\tfrac12\log_a(2\cdot3^2\cdot a)=\boxed{(x+2y+1)/2}$.

## Q18 — Exponential substitution and log domain
(a) $u=2^x>0$. Then $u^2-6u+8=(u-2)(u-4)=0$, giving $u=2,4$ and $\boxed{x=1\text{ or }2}$.
(b) $\log_2x+\log_2(x-2)=3$ has domain $x>2$. Product law gives $x(x-2)=8\Rightarrow(x-4)(x+2)=0$. Reject $-2$; $\boxed{x=4}$.

## Q19 — Repeated roots, tangency
(a) $2x^2+kx+8=0$ has two equal roots when $\Delta=k^2-64=0$, so $\boxed{k=\pm8}$.
(b) At intersections of line $y=mx-1$ and parabola $y=x^2+3$: $x^2-mx+4=0$. Tangency requires $m^2-16=0$, so $\boxed{m=\pm4}$.
(c) For $m=4$, $(x-2)^2=0\Rightarrow x=2$, and $y=4(2)-1=7$. Point $\boxed{(2,7)}$.

## Q20 — Reconstruct and translate quadratic
(a) Vertex $(3,-2)$ gives $f=a(x-3)^2-2$. Through $(1,6)$: $6=4a-2\Rightarrow a=2$; $\boxed{f(x)=2(x-3)^2-2}$.
(b) $0=2[(x-3)^2-1]\Rightarrow x=2,4$. Intercepts $\boxed{(2,0),(4,0)}$.
(c) $\boxed{f(x)=2(x-2)(x-4)}$.
(d) Translate left 1 and up 3: $g(x)=f(x+1)+3=2(x-2)^2+1=\boxed{2x^2-8x+9}$.
(e) $g(x)=2(x-2)^2+1\ge1>0$, so **no real zeros**. Equivalently its discriminant is $-8<0$.
