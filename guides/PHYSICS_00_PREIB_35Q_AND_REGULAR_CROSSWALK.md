## Urutan pengajaran (tanpa kuis sebelum siswa memilih)

1. **[PRE-IB — PRIORITAS]** Vektor dan konsep gerak: scalar, displacement, speed, velocity, vector difference; contoh dari Q1.4, 1.18, 1.27, 1.28, 1.35. Catatan vektor reguler hanya penguatan.
2. **[PRE-IB — PRIORITAS]** GLB, GLBB, grafik dan gerak bertahap: gradien posisi–waktu=velocity; gradien velocity–waktu=acceleration; luas velocity–waktu=displacement; laju percepatan tidak selalu konstan. Q1.2,1.5,1.6,1.9–12,1.19–20,1.22–23,1.32–33.
3. **[PRE-IB — PRIORITAS]** Vertikal: upward positive $a=-g$, apex, flight time and $s\propto t^2$ from rest. Q1.1,1.11,1.17.
4. **[PRE-IB — PRIORITAS]** Parabola: $u_x=u\cos\theta$, $u_y=u\sin\theta$ for angle from horizontal; $x=u_xt$, $y=y_0+u_yt-\frac12gt^2$, $v_y=u_y-gt$, resultant speed, non-level target, air resistance, complementary angles; Q1.3,1.7–8,1.13–16,1.21,1.24–31,1.34; supplemented by Pre-IB MHTML.
5. **[REGULER — DASAR]** Practice all regular vector/GLB/GLBB/parabola techniques as foundations for Pre-IB, not a separate guaranteed exam specification.
6. **[REGULER — TAMBAHAN, PRE-IB UNCONFIRMED]** Measurement reading/uncertainty, significant figures, scientific notation, dimensional analysis. Teach for completeness when core is secure; don't predict SAS inclusion.
7. **No surprise tests.** Student requested full teaching before any testing.
